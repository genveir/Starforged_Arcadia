# Arcadia: House Rules

Rulings made at the table. These override the rules documents where they conflict. Add new rulings here as they are made. Occasionally they will be rolled into the rest of the rules.

## Alien oracles. 

Starforged oracles that assume human subjects (settlements, characters, factions) are read as alien or mixed by default outside human space.

## Installing modules. 

A new module asset is installed at a spaceyard. Bought through Advance away from a yard, it waits until the ship reaches one.

## Goodwill

Goodwill is a separate track per person, kept for anyone the character spends real time with at home. When the character spends meaningful social time with someone, not working, and the player and GM agree it brings them closer, gain 1 goodwill with that person. There is no limit. Goodwill can be spent two ways. On a task roll where that person could conceivably help, spend any amount for an equal bonus to the roll, on top of any other adds; they help in the fiction. Or spend 10 to mark one box of progress on their connection track. Spending goodwill is calling on someone's willingness, not damaging the relationship.

Goodwill and connection progress measure different things. Goodwill is how much someone enjoys the character's company and is willing to do for him: hanging out at the beach, a long dinner, a shared hobby. Connection progress, through Develop Your Relationship, is trust in the field: knowing how the other will move, relying on them when it counts, trusting them with your life. It is built by standing together under hardship, keeping vows made to each other, and coming through when it mattered. Someone can be a close friend the character would never take into a fight, or a crewmate he trusts completely and never spends an evening with. When something happens and it's unclear which should improve, ask which of the two it builds. Develop Your Relationship's quieter triggers, sharing a profound moment and giving them something of worth, mark connection progress only when they build that kind of trust. Otherwise, the moment is goodwill. A single moment marks one or the other, never both.

### Recording

Goodwill lives in `player-character.md` and nowhere else, as a goodwill list next to the connections list: one line per person with their current goodwill. During a session, keep goodwill gained and spent in the running record. At the end of the session, update the goodwill list in `player-character.md` along with the connections and task progress lists. Spending 10 to mark a box on a connection adds 4 ticks to its track. Add a line for anyone who gains their first goodwill, and keep the line when it is spent down to 0. People documents in `Arcadia.People` do not record goodwill.
