## Session Moves

**Begin a Session** — When you begin a significant session or chapter of play: identify/adjust flagged content, recap last session, envision current situation and intent. Optionally spotlight a danger, opportunity, or hidden insight as a brief vignette; if you do, take +(1)mom.

**End a Session** — When you end a significant session or chapter of play: reflect on events, identify missed progress opportunities. Use Develop Your Relationship if you strengthened a connection. Use Reach a Milestone if you advanced a quest.

---

## Adventure Moves

**Face Danger** — When you attempt something risky or react to an imminent threat, roll +stat.
SH: success. +(1)mom.
WH: success at a cost. Suffer move (-1).
M: fail or momentary success undercut by dire turn. PtP.

---

**Secure an Advantage** — When you assess a situation, make preparations, or attempt to gain leverage, roll +stat.
SH: take both. WH: choose one:
- +(2)mom
- +1 on next action die roll

M: fail or assumptions betray you. PtP.

---

**Gather Information** — When you search for clues, ask questions, analyze evidence, or do research, roll +stat.
SH: helpful, specific discovery. Path forward made clear. +(2)mom.
WH: new insight that complicates your quest. +(1)mom.
M: dire threat or unwelcome truth uncovered. PtP.

---

**Compel** — When you try to persuade someone or make them an offer, roll +stat.
SH: they comply. +(1)mom.
WH: they comply, but with a demand or complication.
M: they refuse or demand a great cost. PtP.

---

**Check Your Gear** — When you check to see if you have a specific helpful item or resource, roll +supply.
SH: you have it. +(1)mom.
WH: you have it, but choose one: Sacrifice Resources (-1) or -(2)mom.
M: you don't have it. Situation grows more perilous. PtP.

---

## Quest Moves

**Swear an iron vow** — When you swear upon iron to complete a quest, write your vow and give it a rank, then roll +heart (+1 if connection, +2 if bond).
SH: emboldened, path is clear. +(2)mom.
WH: determined but uncertain. +(1)mom; envision how you find a path forward.
M: significant obstacle before any progress. Envision it.

---

**Reach a Milestone** — When you make headway in your quest by overcoming a critical obstacle, gaining meaningful insight, completing a perilous expedition, acquiring a crucial item or resource, earning vital support, or defeating a notable foe, mark progress per vow rank.

---

**Forsake Your Vow** — When you renounce your quest, betray your promise, or the goal is lost to you, clear the vow. Envision the failure's impact, choose one or more: Endure Stress / Test Your Relationship with a connection / Discard an asset / someone else pays the price / an enemy gains power / your reputation suffers.

---

**Fulfill Your Vow** — PM. When you reach the end of your quest, perform progress roll.
SH: vow fulfilled. Mark quests legacy reward per rank: troublesome=1 tick; dangerous=2 ticks; formidable=1 box; extreme=2 boxes; epic=3 boxes.
WH: as above, but more remains. Swear a new vow to claim full reward; otherwise reward drops one rank.
M: vow undone. Choose: Forsake Your Vow, or recommit (roll both challenge dice, take lowest, clear that many progress boxes, raise rank by one).

---

## Connection Moves

**Make a Connection** — When you search out a new relationship or give focus to an existing one, roll +heart.
SH: connection made. Assign role and rank. When they aid you on a move matching their role, add +1 and take +(1)mom on a hit.
WH: as above, but with a complication or cost.
M: no connection made. Situation worsens. PtP.

---

**Develop Your Relationship** — When you reinforce your relationship with a connection by swearing a vow in their service, completing a quest for them, leveraging their help in desperation, giving them something of worth, sharing a profound moment, standing with them against hardship, or overcoming a test of your relationship, mark progress per connection rank.

If you already share a bond, do not mark progress. Instead roll +their rank (troublesome=+1 through epic=+5). SH: +2 ticks on bonds legacy. WH: +(2)mom. M: no lasting benefit.

---

**Deepen a Connection** — When you recognize a connection has proven more significant than their rank reflects, increase their rank by one (not available if bonded). Recalculate progress: divide current boxes by old rank's increment, multiply by new rank's increment.

---

**Cool a Connection** — When you choose to withdraw from a relationship with a connection, roll +heart (not available if bonded).
SH: decrease rank by one; recalculate progress: divide current boxes by old rank's increment, multiply by new rank's increment.
WH: as above, but clear all progress at new rank.
M: connection broken. Remove them and PtP.

---

**Forge a Bond** — PM. When your relationship with a connection is ready to evolve, perform progress roll.
SH: bond formed. Mark bonds legacy reward per rank (same scale as Fulfill Your Vow). Choose one: bolster influence (add +2 instead of +1 on role-matched moves) or expand influence (add a second role).
WH: as above, but they ask something first. Do it or Swear an Iron Vow to see it done.
M: conflicting motivation revealed. Recommit (roll both challenge dice, take lowest, clear that many boxes, raise rank by one) or don't.

---

**Test Your Relationship** — When your relationship with a connection is tested through conflict, betrayal, or circumstance, roll +heart (+1 if bonded).
SH: use Develop Your Relationship.
WH: use Develop Your Relationship, but also envision a demand or complication.
M: choose one: lose the connection (envision impact, PtP) or prove loyalty (Swear an Iron Vow, formidable or greater; no benefit from connection until complete; permanent loss if refused or failed).

---

## Exploration Moves

**Undertake an Expedition** — When you trailblaze a route through perilous space, journey over hazardous terrain, or survey a mysterious site, give the expedition a name and rank. For each segment, roll +stat.
SH: reach a waypoint. Mark progress per rank.
WH: as above, but choose one: suffer costs en route (suffer move -2, or two suffer moves -1) or face a peril at the waypoint.
M: waylaid by crisis, or arrive at waypoint to confront immediate hardship. No progress. PtP.

---

**Explore a Waypoint** — When you divert from an expedition to examine a notable location, roll +wits.
SH: choose one (or Make a Discovery on a match): find an opportunity (+(2)mom) or gain progress per expedition rank.
WH: uncover something interesting bound up in peril. +(1)mom.
M: immediate hardship or threat. PtP. (On a miss with a match, may Confront Chaos instead.)

---

**Finish an Expedition** — PM. When your expedition comes to an end, perform progress roll.
SH: destination reached. Mark discoveries legacy reward per rank (same scale as Fulfill Your Vow).
WH: as above, but unforeseen complication; reward drops one rank.
M: destination lost or true cost revealed. Choose: abandon (PtP) or return (roll both challenge dice, take lowest, clear that many boxes, raise rank by one).

---

**Set a Course** — When you follow a known route through perilous space, across hazardous terrain, or within a mysterious site, roll +supply.
SH: arrive; situation favors you. +(1)mom.
WH: arrive but choose one: suffer costs en route (suffer move -2, or two -1) or face a complication at destination.
M: significant threat en route. PtP. If overcome, may push on safely.

---

**Make a Discovery** — When your exploration of a waypoint uncovers something wondrous, envision the nature of the discovery and how it is revealed. You mark 2 ticks on discoveries legacy.

---

**Confront Chaos** — When your exploration of a waypoint uncovers something dreadful, choose 1–3 aspects. Envision the nature of the horror and how it is revealed. For each aspect it embodies, when first confronted, you mark 1 tick on discoveries legacy.

---

## Combat Moves

**Enter the Fray** — When you initiate combat or are forced into a fight, envision your objective and give it a rank. If the combat includes discrete challenges or phases, set an objective with a rank for each. Roll +stat to determine if you start in control.
SH: take both. WH: choose one: +(2)mom or you are in control.
M: you are in a bad spot.

---

**Gain Ground** — When you are in control and take action in a fight to reinforce your position or move towards an objective, roll +stat.
On a hit, stay in control. SH: choose two. WH: choose one:
- Mark progress
- +(2)mom
- +1 on next action die roll

M: foe gains upper hand or new peril emerges. Bad spot. PtP.

---

**React Under Fire** — When you are in a bad spot and take action in a fight to avoid danger or overcome an obstacle, roll +stat.
SH: success; in control. +(1)mom.
WH: success at a troublesome cost. Suffer move (-1). Stay in bad spot.
M: situation worsens. Stay in bad spot. PtP.

---

**Strike** — When you are in control and assault a foe, roll +iron (power) or +edge (precision).
SH: mark progress twice. Stay in control.
WH: mark progress twice. You are in a bad spot.
M: fight turns against you. Bad spot. PtP.

---

**Clash** — When you are in a bad spot and fight back against a foe, roll +iron (power) or +edge (precision).
SH: mark progress twice. In control.
WH: mark progress. Counterblow or setback. Stay in bad spot. PtP.
M: foe dominates. Stay in bad spot. PtP.

---

**Face Defeat** — When you abandon or are deprived of an objective, envision the consequence, clear the objective, and PtP. May create a new objective. If objectives remain, fight continues; you are in a bad spot.

---

**Take Decisive Action** — PM. When you seize an objective in a fight, perform progress roll. *If in a bad spot and progress track is not at 10/10: SH counts as WH, WH counts as M.*
SH: prevail. +(1)mom. If fight continues, in control.
WH: objective achieved at cost. Roll d100 or choose:
- 1–40: worse than you thought — suffer move (-2)
- 41–52: short-lived victory — new peril or foe
- 53–64: collateral damage — something lost or broken
- 65–76: others pay the price
- 77–88: marked for vengeance
- 89–100: true nature of foe or objective revealed

M: defeated or objective lost. PtP.

---

**Battle** — When you fight a battle and it happens in a blur, envision your objective and roll +stat.
SH: objective achieved. You may take +(2)mom.
WH: objective achieved at cost. PtP.
M: defeated or objective lost. PtP.

---

## Suffer Moves

**Roll results on all suffer moves are the result of a resist roll. When no resist roll is made, the only effect is the meter decreasing.**

**Lose Momentum** — When you face a setback, -(1/2/3)mom.

**Endure Harm** — When you face physical injury, fatigue, or illness, suffer -1/2/3 health. Excess at 0 becomes -(X)mom.
Resist: highest of +health, +iron
SH: choose one: shake it off (if not wounded, +1 health) or embrace the pain (+(1)mom).
WH: if not wounded, may -(1)mom for +1 health. Otherwise press on.
M: worse than thought. Suffer -1 health or -(2)mom. If health is 0, also mark **wounded** or **permanently harmed**, or roll:
- 1–10: mortal harm — Face Death
- 11–20: dying — Heal within hours or Face Death
- 21–35: unconscious and out of action
- 36–50: reeling — vigorous activity before a breather triggers another roll
- 51–100: still standing

---

**Endure Stress** — When you face mental strain, shock, or despair, suffer -1/2/3 spirit. Excess at 0 becomes -(X)mom.
Resist: highest of +spirit, +heart
SH: choose one: shake it off (if not shaken, +1 spirit) or embrace the darkness (+(1)mom).
WH: if not shaken, may -(1)mom for +1 spirit. Otherwise press on.
M: worse than thought. Suffer -1 spirit or -(2)mom. If spirit is 0, also mark **shaken** or **traumatized**, or roll:
- 1–10: overwhelmed — Face Desolation
- 11–25: give up — Forsake Your Vow
- 26–50: act against your better instincts
- 51–100: persevere

---

**Withstand Damage** — When your vehicle faces a damaging situation or environment, suffer -1/2/3 integrity. Excess at 0 becomes -(X)mom.
Resist: +integrity
SH: choose one: bypass the damage (if not battered, +1 integrity) or ride it out (+(1)mom).
WH: if not battered, may -(1)mom for +1 integrity. Otherwise press on.
M: worse than thought. Suffer -1 integrity or -(2)mom. If integrity is 0, also:
- **Command vehicle**: mark **battered** or **cursed**, mark a module **broken**, destroy a broken module, or roll on table below. If destroyed: Overcome Destruction.
- **Support vehicle**: mark **battered** or roll on table below. If destroyed: discard asset.
- **Incidental vehicle**: roll on table below.

| d100 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | Catastrophic destruction. All aboard Endure Harm or Face Death. |
| 11–25 | Destruction imminent and unavoidable. Get clear or Face Death. |
| 26–40 | Destruction imminent but avoidable — Repair and raise integrity above 0. Fail: see 11–25. |
| 41–55 | Cannot Repair until Resupply provides a crucial part. Roll again before then: see 11–25. |
| 56–70 | Crippled or out of control. Repair and raise integrity above 0 to recover. |
| 71–85 | Rough ride. All aboard Endure Harm, Endure Stress, or Companion Takes a Hit (-2). |
| 86–95 | Lost fuel, energy, or cargo. Sacrifice Resources (-2). |
| 96–100 | Vehicle holds together. |

---

**Companion Takes a Hit** — When your companion faces physical hardship, suffer -1/2/3 companion health. Excess at 0 becomes -(X)mom.
Resist: +companion's health
SH: companion rallies. +1 health.
WH: if health not 0, may -(1)mom for +1 health. Otherwise press on.
M: worse than thought. -1 health or -(2)mom. If health is 0, companion is out of action until aided. Miss with a match at 0 health: companion is dead or destroyed; discard asset.

---

**Sacrifice Resources** — When you lose or consume resources, suffer -1/2/3 supply. If supply hits 0, mark **unprepared**. Further resource loss while unprepared applies cost to a different suffer move instead.

---

## Threshold Moves

**Face Death** — When you are brought to the brink of death with a chance for recovery or redemption, roll +heart.
SH: cast back into the mortal world.
WH: choose one: die nobly or return with a burden (envision what is revealed, Swear an Iron Vow for an extreme quest, mark **doomed**; clear when quest is complete).
M: you are dead.

---

**Face Desolation** — When you are brought to the brink of desolation, roll +heart.
SH: resist and press on.
WH: choose one: spirit breaks in a noble sacrifice or dire vision (Swear an Iron Vow for an extreme quest, mark **tormented**; clear when quest is complete).
M: lost to despair or horror.

---

**Overcome Destruction** — PM. When your command vehicle is destroyed or irrevocably lost, discard the asset along with any modules and docked support vehicles. If you survive, roll challenge dice against bonds legacy track progress to learn the cost of replacement.
SH: call in a favor, no conditions.
WH: mark **indebted**, Swear an Iron Vow for an extreme quest in their service. Clear when done.
M: as WH, but quest is against your nature, forces Forsake Your Vow on another quest, or serves an enemy.

If you accept the cost: take 1 experience per marked ability on discarded assets (minimum 3). Spend only on new command vehicle, modules, and support vehicles.

---

## Recover Moves

**Sojourn** — When you spend time recovering within a community, roll +heart.
SH: safe refuge. You choose two recover moves (Heal, Hearten, Repair, Resupply), automatic SH on each. A move may be taken more than once.
WH: as above, but one move only.
M: choose one: community needs help or makes a costly demand (comply or Swear an Iron Vow to resolve as SH) or no relief found (PtP).

---

**Heal** — When you receive medical care or provide treatment, roll per situation:
- Receive treatment: +iron
- Mend your own wounds: +iron or +wits (lower)
- Obtain treatment for a companion: +heart
- Provide care: +wits

SH: if wounded, clear impact and +2 health. Otherwise +3 health.
WH: as above, but choose one: -(2)mom or Sacrifice Resources (-2).
M: aid ineffective. PtP.

---

**Hearten** — When you socialize, share intimacy, or find a moment of peace, roll +heart.
SH: if shaken, clear impact and +1 spirit. Otherwise +2 spirit. +1 more if during Sojourn.
WH: as above, but indulgence is fleeting — envision interruption or complication. -(1)mom.
M: no comfort. PtP.

---

**Repair** — When you make repairs to your vehicles, modules, mechanical companions, or other devices, roll per situation:
- Self or companion making repairs: +stat
- Obtain repairs: +supply

On a hit, gain repair points per table. May Sacrifice Resources to buy up to 3 extra points (1 point per -1 supply).

| Situation | SH | WH |
|---|---|---|
| At a facility | 5 | 3 |
| In the field | 3 | 1 |
| Under fire | 2 | 0 |

Spend repair points:
- Clear **battered** on a vehicle: 2
- Fix one **broken** module: 2
- +1 integrity on a vehicle: 1
- +1 health for a mechanical companion: 1
- Repair any other device: 3
- Repair any other device with complication: 2

M: repairs not made. PtP.

---

**Resupply** — When you attempt to bolster your readiness, roll +stat.
SH: choose one: if unprepared, clear impact and +1 supply; otherwise +2 supply — or, if a specific needed item can reasonably be obtained, you acquire it (+(1)mom).
WH: as above, but deal with a cost, complication, or demand first.
M: unexpected peril. PtP.

---

## Legacy Moves

**Earn Experience** — When you fill a box (four ticks) on any legacy track, take 2 experience. Spend via Advance. When a track is fully cleared, restart at 0; future boxes on that track earn 1 experience instead of 2. Progress rolls against a cleared track resolve as if at 10 progress.

---

**Advance** — When you develop your abilities, improve your resources, gain a reward, or boost your influence, spend 3 experience for a new asset or 2 experience to upgrade an existing one. Categories: Module / Support Vehicle / Path / Companion / Deed.

---

## Fate Moves

**Pay the Price** — When you suffer the outcome of an action, make the most obvious negative outcome happen or introduce a serious complication.

---

**Ask the Oracle** — When you seek to resolve questions, discover details, reveal locations, determine how other characters respond, or trigger encounters or events, choose one: draw a conclusion / spark an idea / ask a yes/no question / pick two (rate one as likely, roll to confirm).

| Odds | Yes if you roll... |
|---|---|
| Almost Certain | 11+ |
| Likely | 26+ |
| 50/50 | 51+ |
| Unlikely | 76+ |
| Small Chance | 91+ |
